💭 Truths (20)

1

What is something that starts out hard, gets warm and soft, and feels amazing when you finish? (Answer: Baking bread or a hot bath!)

2

What is something long, flexible, and gets wetter the more you use it? (Answer: A towel or toothbrush!)

3

What is something you love to do under the covers in the dark when nobody is watching? (Answer: Reading with a flashlight or sleeping!)

4

What is something that grows bigger when you touch it gently and care for it? (Answer: A houseplant or garden flower!)

5

What is something that goes in dry and hard, but comes out soft and sticky? (Answer: Chewing gum or pasta!)

6

What is something that has head and tail, but no body? (Answer: A coin!)

7

What gets wetter and wetter the more it dries? (Answer: A bath towel!)

8

What goes up and down but never moves? (Answer: Stairs or temperature!)

9

What has a bed but never sleeps, and runs but never walks? (Answer: A river!)

10

What is something you put in your mouth that is hard when it goes in, but soft when it comes out? (Answer: A hard candy or lollipop!)

11

What is something that gets longer when pulled, fits snuggly, and feels warm? (Answer: A knitted scarf or sweater!)

12

What is something you hold firmly, stroke carefully, and blow on when it is hot? (Answer: A hot cup of coffee or tea!)

13

What is something that fills a room but takes up no physical space? (Answer: Light or music!)

14

What goes in hard and dry, comes out soft and wet, and leaves everyone satisfied? (Answer: A tea bag!)

15

What is something you can grip with both hands, slide in smooth, and pop out with a bang? (Answer: A party popper!)

16

What has four legs, a back, but no arms or legs to walk with? (Answer: A dining chair!)

17

What is something that expands to twice its size when exposed to excitement or heat? (Answer: Popcorn kernels!)

18

What do people love to pop, squeeze, and play with when stressed? (Answer: Bubble wrap!)

19

What is something soft that you slap when you want to make it shape up? (Answer: Pizza dough!)

20

What is something you unwrap, put in your mouth, and enjoy until it dissolves? (Answer: A chocolate truffle!)

🔥 Dares (20)

1

Describe your favorite food using only seductive and dramatic tone of voice.

2

Demonstrate how you eat a banana or ice cream cone using extreme dramatic flair.

3

Say "That’s what she said" after the next 3 statements anyone in the room makes.

4

Wink provocatively at the person sitting across from you every time they speak for 2 rounds.

5

Make a dramatic commercial voiceover for a mundane object in the room like a stapler.

6

Give a 30-second sensual review of a glass of plain water.

7

Say 3 innocent phrases in a sultry whisper voice to the person on your right.

8

Perform a dramatic 15-second slow-motion walk towards the refrigerator.

9

Try to sell a plain pen to the group using ultimate persuasive charm.

10

Do a dramatic hair flip every time you answer a question for the next 5 minutes.

11

Blow a dramatic kiss across the room to the host.

12

Say a cheeky double entendre joke to the group with a completely straight face.

13

Demonstrate how you apply lip balm in extreme slow motion.

14

Give a high-five to the person next to you while maintaining intense eye contact.

15

Make up a 10-second jingle about your favorite snack.

16

Describe a banana using only high-fashion runway terminology.

17

Say the word "spicy" with a dramatic accent after everything you say for 2 rounds.

18

Do a 15-second dramatic pose like a sculpture.

19

Reenact a soap opera gasp and hand gesture at the next thing said.

20

Tell the group why your shoes are the most attractive item in the room.
